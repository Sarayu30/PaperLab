# Research paper previews

`resnet-cvpr-2016-page-1.webp` and `resnet-cvpr-2016-page-2.webp` are reduced-size renders of pages 1 and 2 of:

Kaiming He, Xiangyu Zhang, Shaoqing Ren, and Jian Sun. **Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition**. Proceedings of IEEE CVPR, 2016, pp. 770–778.

Original open-access PDF: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2016/papers/He_Deep_Residual_Learning_CVPR_2016_paper.pdf

Source record: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2016/html/He_Deep_Residual_Learning_CVPR_2016_paper.html

The images preserve the original page content and CVF watermark. They identify and link to the featured research; they are not PaperLab results or a PaperLab notebook. Copyright remains with the original rights holders. The margin question belongs to PaperLab and is displayed separately from the paper image.

Rendered using PDFium at 1.8x, encoded as WebP (quality 86). The complete PDF is not bundled with the app. No external image service is contacted when the preview loads.
